## Posting playbook

- **Timing**: Tuesday–Thursday, 8–10am your audience's timezone.
- **First hour**: reply to every comment — early conversation is what LinkedIn
  amplifies. The "dusty portfolio" question is designed to be easy to answer;
  expect lots of "😅 mine says 2021" replies. Reply to each with warmth.
- **Seed the first comment yourself** with a technical hook:
  "Fun fact: the planet is ~9,400 particles on a Fibonacci sphere — and it
  stops rendering the moment it leaves your viewport. Performance is a
  feature. 🔋"
- **Tag nothing, DM instead**: if you want Intelcia/Woolf colleagues to boost
  it, send them the link directly — early reactions from your network matter
  more than tags.
- **Follow-up post** (1–2 weeks later): "How I built the 3D particle planet"
  breakdown thread — the people who engaged with this one are the audience.
- Update your **LinkedIn Featured section** + headline with the URL same day.
